Soil and Water Conservation
Dam Rehabilitation and Improvement Project (DRIP)
 
OBJECTIVES
✴ To reduce siltation in multipurpose reservoirs.
✴ To prevent land degradation by adoption of soil conservation measures in catchment areas.
✴ To reduce soil erosion and to conserve top soil.
 
FUNDING PATTERN
✴ World Bank aided State plan scheme
 
BENEFITS OFFERED (SUBSIDY)
✴ Soil Conservation works are carried out with 100 % subsidy.
 
PROJECT AREA
✴ Catchment areas of Vaigai dam in Theni district
✴ Mettur dam in Salem district
 
DETAILS OF WORKS
✴ Gabion Check Dams
✴ Check Dams
✴ Stream Bank Protection Walls
✴ Silt Monitoring Stations
Field Bunding with Vegetative measures
 
ELIGIBILITY
✴ All farmers of Vaigai and Mettur dam catchment areas in project areas.
 
OFFICER TO BE APPROACHED
✴ Assistant Executive Engineer (AE)'s in the concerned project districts.
