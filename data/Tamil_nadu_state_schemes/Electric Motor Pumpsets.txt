Water Management
Electric motor pumpsets with subsidy
 
OBJECTIVES
✴ To increase power utilization efficiency.
✴ To pump more irrigation water at lesser cost.
 
FUNDING PATTERN
✴ 60 % Central and 40 % State fund.
 
BENEFITS OFFERED (SUBSIDY)
✴ Subsidy - Rs.15,000/- or 50 % of the cost of electric motor pumpsets whichever is less for purchasing new electric motor pumpsets.
 
PROJECT AREA
✴ All the districts in Tamil Nadu (Except Chennai)
 
DETAILS OF WORKS
✴ Subsidy assistance for purchase of new electric motor pumpsets for replacing the old ones or for the purchase of new electric motor pumpsets for the wells newly created.
 
ELIGIBILITY
✴  All the farmers who are having their own EB connection for the irrigation well.
 
OFFICER TO BE APPROACHED
✴ Assistant Engineer (AE) in the concerned block and the Assistant Executive Engineer (AE)’s in the concerned project Revenue Diviosn level..
