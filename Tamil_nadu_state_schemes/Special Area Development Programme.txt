Soil and Water Conservation
Special Area Development Programme
 
OBJECTIVES
✴ Ecological restoration and conservation by adopting integrated watershed approach in a holistic manner for sustainable livelihood and enhancing agricultural productivity
 
FUNDING PATTERN
✴ State Plan
 
BENEFITS OFFERED (SUBSIDY)
✴ 100 % grant is provided for all soil and water conservation and water management measures in community land.
For individual based works
☆ 90 % subsidy for ST category
☆ 80 % subsidy for SC category
☆ 50 % subsidy for General category.

DETAILS OF WORKS
✴ Gabion check dams.
✴ Check dams.
✴ Landslide protection works.
✴ Stream bank protection wall.
✴ Creation of new irrigation facilities.
 ✴ Renovation of Water Harvesting Structures.
 
ELIGIBILITY
✴ All farmers in the project area.
