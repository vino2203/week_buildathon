  Skill Development Training by Agricultural Engineering Department
 
OBJECTIVES
✴ For improving the economy of the unemployed rural youth , Agricultural Engineering Department is providing training to unemployed rural youth for increasing their employment opportunities
 
ELIGIBILITY
✴ Assistant Tractor Operator Training - Age  between 18 years to 35 years 
✴ Agricultural Machinery Demonstrator - Age  between 18 years to 35 years 
 
PROJECT AREA
✴ All Districts

Training Details

Eligibility

	

Age between 18 - 35




      Training Centres
